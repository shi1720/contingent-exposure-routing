# Research plan and claim discipline

Started 2026-09-23. This is a prospective local experiment plan, not an externally registered protocol.

Working title: Contingent Exposure Routing: Model Failover and Market-Impact Risk.
Author: Shivam Gupta, Independent Researcher.

## Question
Does routing financial decisions to surviving AI endpoints by request count control the financial concentration induced by failover? Can an exposure-based routing criterion provide an auditable improvement while explicitly accounting for indivisible decisions?

## Proposed contribution, conditional on literature and results
An outage-contingent exposure model; a sharp symmetric concentration example; the exact random-routing second moment and a finite-agent rounding certificate; an executable exposure-aware assignment procedure with fractional lower bounds; a reproducible computational evaluation, including real responses to constructed financial arithmetic tasks.

Established ingredients must be credited: fire-sale linearization, portfolio second moments, water filling, conditional-expectation rounding, convex quadratic optimization, and load balancing. No claim to invent those methods, or to prove absence of all prior work.

## Evidence layers
1. Mathematical derivations under explicit assumptions, checked by independent enumeration and numerical identities.
2. Seeded synthetic financial networks and shocks, including controls and failures of the method.
3. Real API response records on synthetic tasks with algebraic ground truth. No claim of empirical market calibration, investment alpha, real outages, or independent AI providers: all tested endpoints use the same API vendor.

## Stop conditions
No fabricated outcomes, invented references, unrun experiments, unsupported novelty or acceptance claims. Negative results remain in the record. Do not represent a draft as accepted or a conference abstract as a peer-reviewed journal article.

## Prior manuscript lessons
The supplied manuscripts are careful about narrow claims but several are primarily audits of established principles or very small models. This project should pair a specific finance question with a usable intervention, explicit finite-population implementation, out-of-sample comparisons, and a precise boundary between theory and operational evidence. Prior PDFs are sources for learning only; they are not modified.
